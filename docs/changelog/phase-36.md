  of different lives (`resources/configs/simulations/personas.yaml`) read

  to add when asked.

  exactly: every act in force checked against its ties - a legal basis
  repealed, a reference to a repealed act (and the act that replaced it),
  amendments gathered since its last consolidated text, a judgment of the
  Constitutional Tribunal with no amendment after it - each finding with
  the improvement it suggests.
- **Improvements proposed**: a model reads the act, its findings, its
  debates in Sejm and its simulations through MCP, and writes what could be
  improved, article by article. Not legal advice.
- **M4 closes** in the ROADMAP.
