    Assumptions in the docs¶

    • Text types. O is the published PDF (one act compared byte for byte). That U is "tekst ujednolicony" and T is "tekst aktu" is read from file names.
    • Dates in listings. That announcementDate is the date of the act and promulgation the date of publication is how the data reads.
    • Copyright. That normative acts are outside copyright is article 4 of the act of 1994, as I recall it. The terms of the API were not found written anywhere.
    • A published PDF never changes, so gather --since does not take it again.
    • Requests for everything: about 572 thousand plus the other texts, from counts in listings on 2026-10-13. Size on disk is unknown; sixteen years took 10 GB.

    Limits known and left¶

    • A month of judgments that is here is not asked for again, so judgments added later to an old month are missed.
    • A file with a record counts as gathered even when it is nowhere. Deleting a file to take it again needs its record deleted too.
    • hub still compares a bucket by path and size. Records make it possible to check files here, and they do not check what is on Hugging Face.
