# lawchain: the chain of the law

Phase 39 of P_JOL_ROLL, *no centre*: a Cosmos SDK chain (v0.53, CometBFT
0.38), scaffolded by [Ignite](https://ignite.com), on which the mirrors of
the law agree on what the law is.

- **The mirrors are the validators.** Each mirror of
  `resources/configs/mirrors/mirrors.yaml` runs a node of this chain and is
  one of its validators.
- **A seal per year.** A mirror records, for each year of a journal it has,
  the root of its records of `task seal`: the sha256 of every file's path and
  checksum, sorted (`pjolchain roots` in the Go stack computes it):
  `lawchaind tx law record-seal DU 2026 <root> <files>`. Only the account of
  a bonded validator may; a mirror's newer seal of a year replaces its
  older one.
- **The agreement.** `lawchaind query law agreement DU 2026` answers the
  root sealed by the most bonded mirrors, and whether they are at least the
  share of all of them (`share`, 67% - two thirds, as CometBFT's own
  consensus asks). A mirror whose files differ is outvoted, and seen.

The module is `x/law`: `keeper/msg_server_record_seal.go`,
`keeper/query_agreement.go`, the seals kept as `(journal, year, mirror) ->
"<root> <files>"`. The rest is Ignite's scaffold of a chain (`app/`, `cmd/`,
`proto/`); `proto/lawchain/law/v1/` defines the message and the query, and
its Go is generated by `ignite generate proto-go`.

```bash
task chain:build            # lawchaind into $GOPATH/bin
task unittesting:chain      # a chain of one mirror: a year sealed and agreed, a stranger refused
task go:check               # with go vet and go test of this module
```

The guide - the genesis of a network of mirrors, a mirror's node in compose
(profile `chain`), `task chain:seal` - is `docs/guides/chain.md`.
